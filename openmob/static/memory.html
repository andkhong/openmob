What OpenMob remembers

Short notes your agent reads before a task: things about you, your apps, and the people you message. Nothing is saved until you say so: the agent can only suggest a note, and you choose Save or Not now. Edit or delete any note at any time. Your agent never types a phone number or email from a note: a person note names someone in your phone's contacts.
